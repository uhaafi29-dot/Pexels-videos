The old you isn't gone. It's waiting where you left it.

It's not just willpower. Habits get tied to the cues around them: the bed, the phone, the time of night. Those cues can start the routine before you've consciously decided anything.

When researchers followed students who transferred to a new university, their old habits broke down in the new setting, except where parts of the old context stayed the same. That's the idea behind habit discontinuity: when the cues change, you get a window to choose again.

Try this tonight, as an experiment: charge your phone outside the bedroom, then notice what's different over the next week.
